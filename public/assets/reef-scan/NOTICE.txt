USNM 229 scan
============
Acropora cytherea (original published name: Madrepora cytherea)
National Museum of Natural History, Smithsonian Institution.
This is a dry museum skeleton, used as a dead-skeleton environment asset.
It is not a scan of a living Acropora muricata individual.

Specific official object 3D media is declared public domain. Smithsonian
Open Access dedicates marked digital assets under CC0. See license-evidence.json
for source URLs, observation scope, and the preserved manifest copyright mismatch.
The original downloaded GLB and document.json have not been modified.
